# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead the technical execution of the team, support delivery health, and help teams balance quality, pace, and capacity. They translate product and project priorities into actionable engineering plans.

### Responsibilities
- Align engineering work with roadmap and milestone commitments
- Help plan staffing, prioritization, and team capacity
- Coach engineers on delivery quality, technical decisions, and execution discipline
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on risk, scope, and trade-offs
- Ensure engineering dependencies and delivery blockers are surfaced early

### Goals
- Deliver predictable, high-quality engineering output
- Maintain healthy team execution and sustainable delivery pace
- Improve cross-functional alignment around technical feasibility and risk

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and staffing discussions
- Weekly delivery reviews with PMs and leadership
- Technical risk and dependency updates in project forums

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers to keep timelines realistic and risks visible
- Collaborates with Product Managers to align roadmap priorities with team capacity
- Supports Developers with coaching, prioritization, and technical decision-making
- Shares dependency and delivery health updates with stakeholders and leadership

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads shape user experience direction and ensure product decisions are grounded in usability, accessibility, and customer needs. They help transform business goals into serviceable user journeys and design decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define UX strategy, interaction patterns, and design quality standards
- Translate customer and stakeholder feedback into user-centered product direction
- Participate in discovery work, usability validation, and design reviews
- Partner with product and engineering on trade-offs and implementation feasibility
- Ensure accessibility, clarity, and consistency in customer-facing experiences

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and product usability
- Reduce rework caused by unclear user experience decisions
- Ensure design quality is visible throughout the delivery lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and discovery sessions
- Cross-functional planning and prioritization discussions
- Usability findings and customer insight updates

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate customer needs and desired outcomes
- Supports Developers by clarifying UX intent and implementation details
- Coordinates with Project Managers to ensure design work is reflected in milestones and delivery plans
- Provides stakeholder-facing insight on usability, accessibility, and customer impact

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads ensure that product changes meet quality standards, acceptance criteria, and release readiness expectations. They help connect engineering output to customer trust and operational stability.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release readiness criteria
- Partner with developers and product teams on acceptance criteria and validation plans
- Review defects, prioritization, and root cause analysis for quality issues
- Coordinate manual and automated validation across key user journeys
- Identify quality risks early and help mitigate them before release

### Goals
- Reduce defects escaping into production
- Improve confidence in release quality and operational readiness
- Support predictable and reliable delivery outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, QA signoff, and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage with engineering and product partners
- Quality risk updates during sprint and milestone reviews

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to clarify expected behavior and test coverage
- Supports Product Managers by validating if work meets acceptance criteria
- Provides Project Managers with release risk and readiness information
- Coordinates with stakeholders on quality expectations and customer impact

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads help teams identify, assess, and mitigate regulatory, privacy, and security risk throughout the lifecycle of a project. They make sure delivery work meets organizational and customer obligations.

### Responsibilities
- Review project work for compliance, security, and privacy risks
- Define required controls, governance checkpoints, and review criteria
- Participate in architecture, release, and incident review discussions
- Partner with engineering teams on remediation plans and mitigation priorities
- Support project leaders in escalating material risks and response actions

### Goals
- Reduce exposure to security and compliance risk
- Ensure delivery decisions are informed by policy, risk, and control requirements
- Support trust, resilience, and operational safety across initiatives

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews, control assessments, and security checkpoints
- Escalation updates for sensitive or high-impact issues
- Security signoff for changes before deployment or release

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk and governance impacts
- Works with Developers on secure design, verification, and remediation planning
- Provides stakeholder visibility into critical risks and approval needs
- Aligns with Engineering Managers on release readiness and control compliance

---

## Customer Success / Support Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Support Leads connect delivery teams with customer feedback, service experience, and operational realities. They help ensure that product decisions reflect real customer needs and support outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Surface customer pain points, feature requests, and support trends
- Translate support issues into backlog opportunities and delivery priorities
- Coordinate with product and engineering teams on customer-impacting fixes
- Help triage business impact, urgency, and customer communication needs
- Share operational learning back into planning and roadmap discussions

### Goals
- Improve customer trust, retention, and satisfaction
- Convert service feedback into actionable product improvements
- Ensure customer impact is visible in planning and prioritization

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback, issue review, and support trend updates
- Cross-functional prioritization and escalation discussions
- Stakeholder updates on customer impact and mitigation plans

### Interaction with existing roles
- Feeds Product Managers with customer evidence and success signals
- Partners with Project Managers to prioritize customer-impact work and escalations
- Supports Developers with real-world examples of issues and opportunities
- Coordinates with stakeholders on communication and expectation setting

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads help teams define and track the signals that matter most to success. They turn operational and customer data into actionable learning for decision-making and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, tracking plans, and reporting needs
- Validate whether product changes are delivering expected outcomes
- Support experimentation, measurement, and dashboard creation
- Help interpret trends, anomalies, and customer behavior patterns
- Share evidence with product and leadership teams for course correction

### Goals
- Improve decision-making with measurable evidence
- Standardize how project impact is tracked and communicated
- Support learning and iteration based on real outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Metric reviews and outcome reporting
- Dashboard and experiment updates
- Decision-support briefings for PMs and stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to define measurable success criteria
- Supports Project Managers with milestone progress and outcome tracking
- Helps Engineering Managers assess the operational impact of technical changes
- Provides stakeholders with clear evidence of value delivered or risks uncovered
